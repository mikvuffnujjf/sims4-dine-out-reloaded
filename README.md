## Overview

Dine Out Reloaded is a comprehensive fix and AI overhaul for The Sims 4 Dine Out game pack, correcting the long-standing bugs that caused staff to stand idle, customers to leave without eating, chefs to ignore order queues, and revenue calculations to malfunction. After applying this mod, restaurant simulation works as originally intended.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **The Sims 4 Dine Out Reloaded** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.
